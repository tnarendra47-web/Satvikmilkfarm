What is Gir cow milk?

Gir cow milk is milk from the Gir breed, a native Indian (desi) cow from Gujarat's Saurashtra region. The Gir is one of India's best-known indigenous dairy breeds. You can tell it by its domed forehead, long drooping ears and red-to-speckled coat.

Our herd has Gir cows, HF (Holstein Friesian) cows and buffaloes. That is why our shop lists Gir cow milk separately from full fat milk and buffalo milk.

Is Gir cow milk A2 milk?

Gir is a native Indian breed, and native breeds are generally associated with the A2 type of beta-casein protein. Satvik has not published a lab test of its own milk's A2 status. Here is where things stand:

  • A1 and A2 are two forms of beta-casein, one of the proteins in milk. Many native Indian breeds such as Gir are reported to carry mainly the A2 form.
  • In August 2024 FSSAI issued an advisory asking food businesses to remove "A1" and "A2" claims from labels, and then withdrew the advisory a few days later (26 August 2024) for further consultation.
  • Researchers still disagree about whether A2 milk is easier to digest. We don't make health claims for our milk. If you have a medical condition or are choosing milk for an infant, please ask your doctor.

Until a lab report is published, this page describes the milk as Gir breed milk only and does not claim "certified A2".

Gir cow milk or buffalo milk: which should I order?

Both cost ₹80 per litre at Satvik. Pick buffalo milk if you want something thicker and creamier, or Gir cow milk if you prefer a lighter desi-cow milk. Buffalo milk is generally higher in fat than cow milk, which is why many families use it for malai, curd and paneer at home. Cow milk is generally lighter.
