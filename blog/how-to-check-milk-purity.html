How to Check Milk Purity at Home: Simple FSSAI Tests

By Satvik Milk Farm, Kumbhariya, Borlai (Pardi taluka, Valsad) · Updated 9 November 2026

Quick answer: FSSAI's DART (Detect Adulteration with Rapid Test) guide gives three easy home checks for milk: a drop test for added water, a shake test for detergent and an iodine test for starch. They are quick screening tests, not proof. For a reliable result, use an FSSAI-notified or NABL-accredited food lab. No home test can tell you the cow's breed (Gir) or whether milk is A2.

Families in Vapi, Valsad and Pardi often ask us how to check whether their milk is pure, or how to identify Gir cow milk (ગીર ગાયના દૂધની ઓળખ, दूध में मिलावट की पहचान). This guide sums up what India's food regulator, FSSAI, suggests you can do at home, and what you cannot find out without a lab.

3 FSSAI home tests for milk adulteration

What you are checkingHow to test (FSSAI DART)What the result means
Added waterPut a drop of milk on a polished slanting surface (for example a clean steel plate held at an angle).Pure milk stays or flows slowly, leaving a white trail. Milk with added water flows immediately without leaving a mark.
DetergentMix 5–10 ml of milk with an equal amount of water and shake well.A dense lather suggests detergent. Pure milk forms only a very thin layer of foam.
StarchTake 2–3 ml of milk and add 2–3 drops of tincture of iodine (for milk, FSSAI says you do not need to add water or boil).A blue colour indicates starch. No blue colour means no starch was detected by this test.

Source: FSSAI, DART — Detect Adulteration with Rapid Test, milk & milk products section (eatrightindia.gov.in/dart). FSSAI also publishes video demonstrations of these and other tests, including urea in milk, on fssai.gov.in.

What home tests cannot tell you

  • They are not lab results. FSSAI describes DART as an awareness manual for households. A negative home test does not prove milk is safe, and a doubtful result should be confirmed by a food lab.
  • They cannot identify the breed. There is no kitchen test for Gir cow milk, and no home test for A1 or A2 milk. Colour, taste or the thickness of cream are not reliable signs of breed, because they change with feed, season, fat content and handling.
  • They do not measure fat or SNF. FSSAI sets minimum fat and SNF (solids-not-fat) for each milk class, such as cow, buffalo, full cream and skimmed milk. Checking these needs a lab or a dairy milk analyser. Our cow vs buffalo milk guide and full-fat vs skimmed milk guide explain the classes.
  • Handle chemicals carefully. Use only the simple tests above at home. Leave tests that need strong acids or reagents to labs.

Practical ways to choose pure milk

  1. Know your source. Buy from a seller who tells you which milk you are getting (cow, buffalo, full fat or skimmed) and where it comes from.
  2. Check the price makes sense. Compare like with like. Our milk price in Gujarat guide sets packet milk rates next to farm-fresh prices.
  3. Store it well. Keep milk covered and chilled, and use it quickly.
  4. Report suspected adulteration to your local food safety officer or through FSSAI's official consumer channels.

Frequently asked questions

How can I check milk purity at home?

FSSAI's DART (Detect Adulteration with Rapid Test) guide lists simple household tests: a drop test on a polished slanting surface for added water, a shake test for detergent (dense lather), and an iodine test for starch (blue colour). These are screening tests only; for a reliable result, get the milk tested at an FSSAI-notified or NABL-accredited lab.

How do I check if water is added to milk?

Put a drop of milk on a polished slanting surface. As per FSSAI DART, pure milk either stays or flows slowly, leaving a white trail. Milk with added water flows immediately without leaving a mark.

How do I check for detergent in milk?

Mix 5 to 10 ml of milk with an equal amount of water and shake well. If detergent is present, a dense lather forms; pure milk forms only a very thin layer of foam (FSSAI DART).

Can I identify Gir cow milk or A2 milk at home?

No. No home test can tell the breed of the cow or whether milk is A2. That needs a laboratory test. Ask the seller where the milk comes from; Satvik Milk Farm lists Gir Cow fresh Milk separately from Buffalo Milk, Full Fat Milk and Skimmed Milk on its shop page.

ઘરે દૂધની શુદ્ધતા કેવી રીતે તપાસવી?

FSSAI ના DART મુજબ: દૂધનું એક ટીપું ચીકણી ઢાળવાળી સપાટી પર મૂકો — શુદ્ધ દૂધ ધીમે વહે છે અને સફેદ નિશાન છોડે છે, પાણીવાળું દૂધ તરત વહી જાય છે. દૂધ અને પાણી સરખા ભાગે હલાવો — ઘટ્ટ ફીણ બને તો ડિટર્જન્ટ હોઈ શકે. આયોડિનના 2-3 ટીપાંથી વાદળી રંગ થાય તો સ્ટાર્ચ છે. ચોક્કસ પરિણામ માટે લેબ ટેસ્ટ કરાવો.

घर पर दूध में मिलावट कैसे पहचानें?

FSSAI के DART के अनुसार: दूध की एक बूंद चिकनी ढलान वाली सतह पर रखें — शुद्ध दूध धीरे बहता है और सफेद निशान छोड़ता है, पानी मिला दूध तुरंत बह जाता है। दूध और पानी बराबर मात्रा में मिलाकर हिलाएं — घना झाग बने तो डिटर्जेंट हो सकता है। आयोडीन की 2-3 बूंदों से नीला रंग आए तो स्टार्च मिला है। पक्के नतीजे के लिए लैब टेस्ट कराएं।
