How to compare packet milk and farm-fresh milk fairly

  • Compare the same type. Buffalo with buffalo (Amul Buffalo ≈ ₹78/L vs Satvik Buffalo ₹80), full cream with full fat (Amul Gold ≈ ₹70/L vs Satvik Full Fat ₹70). Toned milk such as Taaza has less fat than full cream milk, so it is cheaper.
  • Breed-specific milk is a separate category. Packet cow milk is usually not labelled by breed. Gir cow milk comes from one Indian breed, and breed-specific milk is generally priced higher than mixed cow milk.
  • Check the legal minimums. FSSAI sets minimum fat and SNF (solids-not-fat) for each milk class, for example buffalo, cow, full cream, toned and skimmed milk. A label name tells you the class it must meet. Our cow vs buffalo milk guide explains the differences.
  • Count delivery and pack size. A 500 ml pouch price doubled is not always the same as a 1 L price. Ask about delivery in your area before comparing a monthly bill.
  • Diet or medical needs: ask your doctor or dietitian. We do not diagnose or prescribe.

Why does farm-fresh milk cost more than packet milk?

Prices depend on the milk type, the breed, fat content, scale and delivery. Large cooperatives like Amul collect milk from many farmers and process it at scale. A small farm supplying households directly has different costs. Compare the same milk type (for example buffalo with buffalo, or full cream with full cream) when you compare prices.
