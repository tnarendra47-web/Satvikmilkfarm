Chaas vs masala chaas

  • Chaas (plain buttermilk) is curd whisked with water into a thin drink. It is ₹5 less per litre on our shop, and you can add your own salt, jeera or herbs at home.
  • Masala Chaas is the same drink with seasoning already added, ready to pour. If you want to know the exact seasoning we use, ask us on WhatsApp before ordering.
  • Chaas vs lassi: chaas is thin and usually savoury; lassi is thicker. Our shop lists Lassi [250ml] ₹25 (was ₹40) and Malai Lassi [250ml] ₹30 (was ₹40).

Which one you pick is a matter of taste. If you have a specific dietary need, ask your doctor or dietitian.

Making chaas at home from our curd

If you prefer to make it yourself, whisk our Full Fat Curd [1kg] ₹75 or Non Fat Curd [1kg] ₹65 with water to the thickness you like, then season to taste. Storage: keep chaas covered in the fridge and finish it soon after opening.

What is the difference between chaas and masala chaas?

Plain chaas is buttermilk made by whisking curd with water. Masala chaas is the same drink with added seasoning such as salt and spices. Message us on WhatsApp if you want to know the exact seasoning we use before ordering.

Is chaas the same as lassi?

No. Chaas is thin and usually savoury, while lassi is a thicker curd drink. Our shop lists Lassi [250ml] at ₹25 and Malai Lassi [250ml] at ₹30, sold in 250 ml packs, while chaas is sold by the litre.
