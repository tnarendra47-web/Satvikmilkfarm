Full fat curd vs non fat curd

  • Full Fat Curd keeps the cream of the milk, so it is richer and thicker.
  • Non Fat Curd is lower in fat, lighter, and ₹10 less per kg on our shop.
  • What FSSAI says: under the Food Safety and Standards regulations, dahi (curd) has the same minimum milk fat and solids-not-fat as the milk it is made from. So the fat level of curd follows the milk used, just as with full-fat and skimmed milk.

Which one is better is a matter of taste and how you use it. If you have a specific dietary need, ask your doctor or dietitian.
