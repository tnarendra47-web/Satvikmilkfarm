How is buffalo milk different from cow milk?

Buffalo milk is usually thicker and creamier than cow milk because it generally has more fat and total solids. India's food regulator, FSSAI, reflects this in its milk standards: the minimum fat required for milk sold as buffalo milk is higher than for cow milk.

  • Texture: buffalo milk is richer and whiter; cow milk is lighter and slightly yellowish.
  • In the kitchen: many Gujarati and North Indian households pick buffalo milk for thick malai, firm dahi, paneer and sweets. Cow milk is often chosen for everyday drinking.
  • Calories: because of the higher fat, a glass of buffalo milk usually has more calories than the same glass of cow milk.

We don't make health claims for our milk. If you have a medical condition, or are choosing milk for a baby or young child, please ask your doctor.
