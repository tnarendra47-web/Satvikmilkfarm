Lassi vs malai lassi

  • Lassi is curd blended into a thick, smooth drink. It is ₹5 less per 250 ml on our shop.
  • Malai Lassi is the richer version, served with malai (cream). If you want to know the exact recipe or how sweet it is, ask us on WhatsApp before ordering.
  • Lassi vs chaas: lassi is thick and sold in 250 ml packs; chaas is thin buttermilk sold by the litre. Our shop lists Chaas [1 liter] ₹25 (was ₹30) and Masala Chaas [1 liter] ₹30.

Which one you pick is a matter of taste. If you have a specific dietary need, ask your doctor or dietitian.

As one reference point, BigBasket and Zepto listed Amul Lassi 250 ml at MRP ₹25 when we checked on 12 Nov 2026. Packet prices differ by shop, app, flavour and city, so check the MRP on the pack. Compare like with like: plain with plain, flavoured with flavoured, and 250 ml with 250 ml.

Making lassi at home from our curd

If you prefer to make it yourself, blend our Full Fat Curd [1kg] ₹75 or Non Fat Curd [1kg] ₹65 with a little chilled water or milk to the thickness you like, and sweeten or salt to taste. Storage: keep lassi covered in the fridge and finish it soon after opening.
